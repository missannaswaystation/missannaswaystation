What do you do when you look out your door and see all these wonderful souls enjoying life in the pastures?

13 October 2026

You stop. You take a deep breath. And you thank God for every single one of them.

Because behind every beautiful face is a story. Some came here broken, some came here scared, some came here forgotten, and some came way too close to losing everything.

But look at them now!

They are grazing, playing, resting in the sunshine, making friends, and doing what every horse deserves to do… JUST BE A HORSE!

This is why we do what we do at Miss Anna’s Way Station. The long days, the sleepless nights, the vet bills, the feed bills, and all the worry suddenly make sense when you look out that door and see PEACE.

They aren’t just horses and donkeys. They are souls who deserved another chance, and we are blessed to be the ones who get to give it to them.

Sometimes people ask why we keep doing this when it’s so hard. All we have to do is look out the door.

THIS is why. THIS is what rescue looks like. THIS is what love looks like.

And to our wonderful horse family who helps us keep hay in front of them, feed in their bellies, and veterinary care available when they need it — you are part of every single happy moment in these pastures, too.

From our hearts to yours, THANK YOU for helping us give these precious souls the life they deserve.

Welcome to Miss Anna’s Way Station, where every soul matters and every life is worth saving.

If this work matters to you, a donation pays for it, and supplies from our wish lists arrive without anyone shopping.
