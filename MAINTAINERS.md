## Why there are two

The organization owns this website. The person who builds it is replaceable; the
asset is not. If the maintainer is unreachable, the second owner must be able to
get in, change a donation link, or hand the work to someone else.

## To add the second owner

1. The person creates a free GitHub account and turns on two-factor authentication
   (GitHub requires it for organization owners).
2. Organization page -> People -> Invite member -> their username.
3. Then: Organization page -> People -> their name -> Role -> Owner.

## If the site breaks

- Site down or page blank: check github.com/miss-anna-s-way-station/missannaswaystation/actions
  and the repository's Settings -> Pages.
- A donation link stopped working: open the organisation's own account (PayPal, Amazon,
  Chewy, Cash App) and generate a fresh link, then update it in `donate.html` and in the
  header of all five pages.
- Nobody available who can code: the site is plain HTML. Any web developer can take it
  over in an afternoon, and the instructions above are the whole of it.

## Domain

The site currently publishes at a `github.io` address. A custom domain can be added at
any time: buy it in the organization's name (not the maintainer's), then point it at
GitHub Pages and add it under Settings -> Pages -> Custom domain.
