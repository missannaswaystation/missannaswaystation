What we do

  • Rescue and intake. We take in neglected, senior and slaughter-bound horses and donkeys, from horses pulled out of the sale barns to foals too sick to stand on their own. Every new arrival spends their first weeks in a quarantine pen, watched closely, before joining the herd.
  • Daily care and feeding. Every day starts with hay and feed and a walk through the fields: checking legs, giving medications, re-wrapping wounds, and making sure every belly is full.
  • Veterinary care. Vet bills are our biggest cost. Dr. Mike at Pallone Veterinary Hospital treats our horses, from wound care and leg wraps to nursing foals through strangles.
  • Lifelong sanctuary. Our horses are home for good. They live out their days here with their herd, with rehabilitation, comfort and the dignity they deserve.

How we are funded

Entirely by donations, and by supplies sent from our wish lists. We are a 501(c)(3) organization, EIN 41-3565519, so gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.
